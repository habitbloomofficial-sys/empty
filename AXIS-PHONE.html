AXIS

One key and he's awake. Axis needs a Google Gemini key to think with. It's free, it takes about a minute, and no card is asked for.

Get one here — sign in with Google, press Create API key, copy it, paste it below.

Your key stays on this phone. It's sent to Google when he answers you, and to nowhere else. There is no server in this file — it's the whole app, and it's talking straight to Google from your hand.

This Axis can talk, think, remember, listen, speak, and search the web. He can't open apps, read your email or touch your computer — that Axis lives on the machine at home and a web page can't reach into it.

What he can't do from here

Open Spotify, read your Gmail, run your automations, open websites on your PC, place calls. All of that is the Axis on your computer doing things to your computer. This one is a conversation, a memory, a voice and the web — which is most of what you want on a train.
